P01 & policy & What is the attendance requirement to sit in the Major exam? & \textit{answer}: At least 75\% attendance in each course (full attendance expected); below 75\% without valid reason the instructor can recommend de-registration (Circular 51, 2024, supersedes the old rule). \\
P02 & policy & What happens if I am absent from all classes for four weeks in a semester? & \textit{answer}: Absence from all academic activities for 4+ weeks (with or without leave) results in automatic semester withdrawal / cancellation of registration. \\
P03 & policy & I missed a minor exam because I was sick. How do I apply for a make-up exam? & \textit{answer}: Make-up is for a missed Minor; submit the Makeup/I-grade application (Annexure A) with a medical certificate issued/verified by the IITJ Medical Officer to the Office of Academic Affairs within 10 days. \\
P04 & policy & What should I do if I miss the Major exam due to illness? & \textit{answer}: Apply for an I grade (not a make-up); the I grade application must reach the Office of Academic Affairs before the last day of the examinations, with a verified medical certificate. \\
P05 & policy & What is the maximum duration allowed to complete a B.Tech programme? & \textit{answer}: 14 registered semesters (2019 Academic Regulations \S{}11.4; supersedes the older 8-year rule). \\
P06 & policy & How many examinations are held for a course in a semester? & \textit{answer}: One Minor and one Major examination (Circular 52, Jul 2024; Examination Guidelines 2024). \\
P07 & policy & When are the minor examinations for Semester I of 2026-27? & \textit{answer}: 15-20 September 2026 (tentative). \\
P08 & policy & What topics are covered in the Autonomous Systems course? & \textit{answer}: Autonomous/semi-autonomous systems (mobile robots, cars): e.g. navigation, obstacle avoidance, planning, multi-agent systems, game theory. \\
P09 & policy & What is the maximum duration allowed to complete a PhD? & \textit{answer}: 14 registered semesters, which may include up to 6 semesters of Registration kept Alive (RKA) (PG Regulations 2022 onwards, \S{}11.5). \\
